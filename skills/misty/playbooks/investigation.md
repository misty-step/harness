# Investigation

1. **Frame the question.** Name the decision or explanation sought, the relevant DOMAIN.md terms and the outside observation that would settle it. **Complete when:** the question is answerable and its evidence boundary is explicit.
2. **Read primary evidence.** Use the `research` skill; inspect the current source, runtime, records or authoritative documentation before secondary accounts. Dispatch read-only subagents through your harness's mechanism only for independent bodies of evidence, with misty's briefing contract. **Complete when:** each consequential premise has a source or is explicitly unverified.
3. **Discriminate.** Compare explanations or options against the outside observation. Keep the work read-only; a code change belongs to the bug-fix or feature playbook. **Complete when:** evidence supports one answer, or the remaining uncertainty and the observation needed to resolve it are precise.
4. **Answer.** Use the `writing` skill; give the conclusion, mechanism or tradeoffs, direct sources and unresolved limit. **Complete when:** the answer follows the core's claim rule, cited links were read, and the recommendation answers the original question without an unrequested implementation.
