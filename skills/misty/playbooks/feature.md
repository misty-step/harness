# Feature

1. **Shape.** Load the `grill` skill where intent is unsettled and the `research` skill for unfamiliar contracts. Name the consumer's outcome, terms, Scope and Keep, and Oracle. **Complete when:** the brief satisfies misty's contract and the outside check has an exact command or executable journey.
2. **Choose the shape.** Read the `principle-deeper-not-bigger` skill, the `principle-deep-modules` skill and, for consequential state, the `principle-model-the-domain` skill. Configure or reuse before building. Route visible work through the `design` skill. **Complete when:** the data shape and interface are named and the chosen design removes, rather than spreads, special cases.
3. **Slice and assign.** Apply the `principle-vertical-slices` skill and misty's delegation boundary and briefing contract. **Complete when:** each unit has an owner, owned paths and its own Oracle, and every brief without one has returned to shaping.
4. **Build.** Implement the smallest complete unit; migrate callers and remove obsolete paths. Use the `test-audit` skill at changed test boundaries. **Complete when:** the specified end-to-end behavior exists without placeholders, duplicate facts or speculative scope.
5. **Prove and ship.** Drive the `verification` skill on the real artifact, then the `ship-loop` skill. **Complete when:** the outside check passes, independent review covers the unit, and the delivery links observed results, design choices and open decisions under the core's claim rule.
