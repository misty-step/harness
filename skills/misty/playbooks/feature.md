# Feature

1. **Shape.** Load `skill://grill` where intent is unsettled and `skill://research` for unfamiliar contracts. Name the consumer's outcome, terms, Scope and Keep, and Oracle. **Complete when:** the brief satisfies misty's contract and the outside check has an exact command or executable journey.
2. **Choose the shape.** Read `skill://principle-deeper-not-bigger`, `skill://principle-deep-modules` and, for consequential state, `skill://principle-model-the-domain`. Configure or reuse before building. Route visible work through `skill://design`. **Complete when:** the data shape and interface are named and the chosen design removes, rather than spreads, special cases.
3. **Slice and assign.** Apply `skill://principle-vertical-slices` and misty's delegation boundary and briefing contract. **Complete when:** each unit has an owner, owned paths and its own Oracle, and every brief without one has returned to shaping.
4. **Build.** Implement the smallest complete unit; migrate callers and remove obsolete paths. Use `skill://test-audit` at changed test boundaries. **Complete when:** the specified end-to-end behavior exists without placeholders, duplicate facts or speculative scope.
5. **Prove and ship.** Drive `skill://verification` on the real artifact, then `skill://ship-loop`. **Complete when:** the outside check passes, independent review covers the unit, and the delivery links observed results, design choices and open decisions under the core's claim rule.
