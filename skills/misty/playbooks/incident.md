# Incident

1. **Warn and bound impact.** Report the live user-facing break immediately in the authorized owner channel. Preserve the failing observation, affected release and accepted-data risk; follow the core's ask-first boundary for third-party messages or production-data writes. **Complete when:** the owner has the consumer impact, known scope and current recovery action under the core's claim rule.
2. **Restore.** Load the `release-watch` skill; use the repository's compatible rollback or explicitly mark the feature unavailable. Escalate before a rollback that risks accepted data. **Complete when:** the recovery action has a receipt or its exact blocker is reported while unblocked containment continues.
3. **Verify recovery.** Load the `verification` skill and drive the formerly failing journey on the live release, including rejection and recovery where affected. **Complete when:** user recovery is observed on the real path; deployment success alone is not the recovery claim.
4. **Repair the cause.** Follow the bug-fix playbook at [bug-fix.md](bug-fix.md). Exercise the alert and rollback path before trusting them. **Complete when:** the cause is repaired, the regression check exposes the defect and the recovery mechanism has observed evidence.
5. **Record the lesson.** For users hurt or accepted data at risk, write a short repository postmortem with impact, timeline, cause, recovery and the smallest prevention; record harmless failures on their PR or ticket. A secret printed only in a local agent session is not an incident and does not authorize rotation. **Complete when:** the linked record and residual risk are visible to the owner without widening the incident's scope.
