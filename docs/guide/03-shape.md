# 3. Shape

Bring a problem, an existing initiative or an uncertain design to [grill](../../skills/grill/SKILL.md). It resolves intent before implementation and produces work that can be checked outside the builder's own judgment.

Keep the whole outcome in view. A small delivery slice is useful on its own; it is not a smaller promise disguised as the finished initiative. If the raw evidence or outside check is missing, shape that part before dispatching a builder.

Record intent and publish tickets through the
[Entire forge procedure](../../skills/misty/entire.md). It owns backlog scope
and dependencies; the spec and stories keep their existing homes.

Use [research](../../skills/research/SKILL.md) for unfamiliar contracts and [design](../../skills/design/SKILL.md) for interfaces. Use [writing](../../skills/writing/SKILL.md) for the resulting prose and [agent-design](../../skills/agent-design/SKILL.md) when the deliverable instructs agents.
