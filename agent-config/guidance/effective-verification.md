## Effective verification

- Distinguish each claimed behavior from a plausible failure. Use expectations independent of the target logic and bind evidence to the exact candidate.
- Use `skill://test-audit` for test authoring or pruning. Here, preserve independent structural, fixture, interaction, snapshot, round-trip, characterization, and scoped smoke evidence when it supports the stated claim.
- Treat semantic findings as advisory. `no_finding` is not proof, and unavailable or missing context cannot authorize completion or weaken deterministic gates.
- Isolated children must receive the effective-verification mandate and exact acceptance criteria in their task context. Skill availability or a board comment is not delivery.
