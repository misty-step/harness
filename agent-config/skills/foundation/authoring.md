# Authoring the foundations

The catalog (`foundation-standard-v1.json`) owns structured obligations;
`constitution.md` states intent, and ADRs keep consequential decisions.
Phaedrus approves new or widened obligations and removal of approved escapes.
Rationale edits and retiring unearned checks use normal review (ADR-006).

Do not turn gaps between old prose and the catalog into a compliance backlog.
Change the owner of the actual rule; do not add a second policy layer.
