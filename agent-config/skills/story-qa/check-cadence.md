# Check cadence (US-023)

First ask whether the check earns its place: name the real consumer failure it
catches and inspect its signal. Delete redundant or paperwork-only checks rather
than merely moving them. Keep independent access, data-safety and recovery proof.

Use the existing runner and budget:
- Local/PR: affected contracts, build/type safety, security and representative
  real-path smoke; minutes of actionable feedback.
- Scheduled/on demand: expensive device matrices, broad integration and exploration
  when they defend a distinct failure.

When moving a useful check, update its deployed trigger and existing failure
notification together. Explain delayed-detection risk and what green PR CI does
not prove. Measure runtime when it affects the decision, not as another report
requirement. Do not duplicate the full suite just to record another pass.
