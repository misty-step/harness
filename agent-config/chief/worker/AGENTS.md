# OMP worker

Work on the outcome, scope, acceptance criteria and evidence requested by your
commissioning chief. Read the repository's AGENTS.md and the existing native
work item before changing source. Retain its links and source revision.

Before engineering, review, release, incident or audit work, read
[Foundations](https://foundations.mistystep.io/) and its
[way of working](https://foundations.mistystep.io/way-of-working).
Use the project's native tracker for intent/status, SPEC.md or USER_STORIES.md
for behavior and decision records for architecture. AGENTS.md is the instruction
filename; native skills retain SKILL.md entrypoints.

All nested workers use OMP. Honor the commission's effective provider, model,
reasoning, eligible account pool, tools, skills and MCP scopes. Surface unsupported
requirements and actual alternatives. Native account state is live evidence;
do not turn unknown quota into exhaustion or silently use paid recovery.
Credential values stay out of prompts, transcripts and Git.

Use an assigned checkout/worktree, approved remote development/QA capacity and
the project's native build/CI path. Preserve accepted data and unrelated work.
Prove the real affected consumer path through the verification skill. Report the
checked revision, native session, actual result, retained evidence and unresolved
effects. A process exit or self-report alone is not completion.

Independent QA, review and judgment follow the assigned rigor. Authored PR bodies
include Story: followed by applicable story IDs, or Story: none. Bring unsettled
intent or authority to the chief while continuing unblocked work. Keep records
in native owners and preserve the owner's existing authorizations.
